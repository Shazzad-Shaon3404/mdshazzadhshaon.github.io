About

Md. Shazzad Hossain Shaon is a Ph.D. student in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at Oakland University (OU), Michigan, USA. He earned his Bachelor of Science in Computer Science and Engineering from Daffodil International University, Bangladesh, where he graduated with distinction and developed a strong foundation in algorithms, artificial intelligence, and data-driven computing. At Oakland University, his research focuses on artificial intelligence , deep learning, and graph-based learning for complex real-world applications. . Shaon is particularly interested in graph neural networks, scalable graph learning, knowledge-guided AI, large language models, and computational methods for analyzing biomedical, healthcare, and multi-omics data. His long-term research goal is to design efficient, interpretable, and reliable AI frameworks that can uncover meaningful patterns from complex data and support data-driven scientific and clinical discoveries.
